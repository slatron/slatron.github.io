# A New Character Center

For season 2, one of the main threads was Sheridan's character development. From a character standpoint, he. was coming in at a serious disadvantage. Most of the cast were defined in terms of their relationship to Sinclair.

JMS spent most of Season One building up the world of B5, using Sinclair as a foil for everyone. To Garibaldi, he was both a hero to look up to and a cautionary tale of what can happen to soldiers who survive. To Delann he was a beacon to both follow and to point others to as the mystery of his relationship to the Mimbari is slowly revealed. To G'Kar, he represents an inward path to respectability for Narn within the coalition of worlds.

<div class="image-plus-caption">
<img src="/ms_assets/images/bab5/ep5b/sinclair.jpg" alt="Sinclair will party">
<em>Whadaya know... I'm I beacon of respectability.</em>
</div>

Sheridan was stepping into an impossible task. Within the plot of the show, he is taking over leadership B5 along with the massive responsibilities there. In the meta-narrative of developing the story, Sheridan has to replace the show's guiding center. Not only does he need to introduce himself to the audience and the other characters, but he has to establish why he is capable of being the guiding center to all these relationships in motion.

# Effects on Season 2

Because of this unforeseen "Changing of the Guard", the first batch of episodes in season 2 feel a bit like we're backtracking. This ended up working out in Sheridan's favor. Sheridan got the chance to have a multi-season character arc. And that's where the Shatner-Picard index comes in.

Within his first two episodes, it's clear Sheridan was picked by the new aggressive isolationist Clark administration because of his military past. Sheridan had the only major victory against the Minbari during the war. The Clark administration assumed they were getting a pro-Earth pawn.

<div class="image-plus-caption small">
<img src="/ms_assets/images/bab5/ep5b/cowboy.png" alt="Cowboy Sheridan">
<em>In my youth, I was also a rustler</em>
</div>

Instead, Sheridan was hand-sewlected behind the scenes by Santiago as a successor to Sinclair. He'll need to come across as Pro-Earth to his superiors, but use tactical diplomacy between multiple factions to achieve his true goal: peace through mutual understanding among alien races.

Sheridan would always be smart enough to present himself as what people needed to see - but was always ahead of everyone and serving a greater agenda. much like Londo staying ahead of the Centauri Senate in seasons 3-4 as he was dealing with Refa and Cartagia.

JMS always had this in mind for Sheridan. <a href="http://www.midwinter.com/lurk/countries/us/guide/027.html" target="_blank">From the Lurker's Guide</a>:

> "...remember the first rule of Babylon 5: nobody is what they appear. Not entirely, anyway There's always something going on, something that somebody's not telling. Some folks are making the error of looking at Sheridan... and thinking 'this is all that he is.' Except, of course, that they weren't and he's not. I would not create a character that is just what you're seeing."

# The Measure of a Captain

This is the thought that led me to developing the Shatner-Picard Index. Every captain can be placed on a spectrum where one extreme is the brash headstrong Shatner type, and the other is the diplomatic and thoughtful Picard.

Note that in Shatner's place, the actor's name is used over the character to demonstrate just how overpowering being a Shatner can be. Just look up how the cast of TOS has to pronounce Sabotage by the force of Shatner's personality.

To measure where Sheridan lies on the scale, I decided to give him a score for each episode in season 2. It would be 1 - 10 points as either a Shatner or a Picard. I kept a running tally over the course of the season. Sheridan is declared a Sheridan or a Picard depending on the larger total. We subtract the lesser total from the greater and the final number becomes the index of how much of a Shatner or Picard the captain is.

<div class="image-plus-caption">
<img src="/ms_assets/images/bab5/ep5b/kirkpicard.jpg" alt="Our Heroes">
<em>Which one R U?</em>
</div>

Shatner points are awarded for irrational behavior, solving problems with fists, going it alone and mating with alien women. Picard points are awarded for solving difficult diplomatic problems, encouraging solutions through working together and sipping tea.

# Other Captains on the Scale

Shatner and Picard are each +10s in their direction on the scale. +10 is also the maximum score a captain can get. For example, Captain Ahab would be a +8 Shatner as the megalomania is not the same, but similar to many Shatner qualities. Captain America gets a +6 Picard as he uses his fists a lot, but is more known for his diplomatic and leadership abilities.

Captain Sinclair presented strongly as a Picard. But his sucka-punchin ways ultimately earned him a +4 Shatner.

Captain Marvel (Batson) would be a +5 Shatner, while Captain Marvel (Danvers) would be a +2 Picard, and Captain Marvel (Rambeau) is a +7 Picard... and Captain Mar-Vell (Lawson) is a +3 Shatner.

You get it.
